# FDA Food Code 2022 §4-602.11: Cleaning frequency for food-contact surfaces

Short verbatim extracts from the FDA Food Code 2022, §4-602.11 "Equipment Food-Contact Surfaces and Utensils" and the related §4-601.11. Capitalised words are terms defined in the Food Code. Priority markers (P, Pf) are left out of the quotes.

## FDA §4-602.11(A): when food-contact surfaces must be cleaned

FDA Food Code 2022, §4-602.11(A) says "Equipment food-contact surfaces and utensils shall be cleaned:"

> "(1) ... before each use with a different type of raw animal FOOD such as beef, FISH, lamb, pork, or POULTRY;"

> "(2) Each time there is a change from working with raw FOODS to working with READY-TO-EAT FOODS;"

> "(3) Between uses with raw fruits and vegetables and with TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD;"

> "(4) Before using or storing a FOOD TEMPERATURE MEASURING DEVICE; and"

> "(5) At any time during the operation when contamination may have occurred."

## FDA §4-602.11(C): clean at least every 4 hours

FDA Food Code 2022, §4-602.11(C) says: "if used with TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD, EQUIPMENT FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES and UTENSILS shall be cleaned throughout the day at least every 4 hours."

Paragraph (D) of §4-602.11 lists exceptions, for example surfaces used in a refrigerated room or area held at set temperatures, with the cleaning frequency documented.

## FDA §4-601.11: what "clean" means

FDA Food Code 2022, §4-601.11(A) says: "EQUIPMENT FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES and UTENSILS shall be clean to sight and touch."

§4-601.11(B) says: "The FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES of cooking EQUIPMENT and pans shall be kept free of encrusted grease deposits and other soil accumulations."
