# FDA Food Code 2022 §4-703.11: Sanitising food-contact surfaces

Short verbatim extracts from the FDA Food Code 2022, §4-702.11 "Before Use After Cleaning", §4-703.11 "Hot Water and Chemical", §4-501.111 "Manual Warewashing Equipment, Hot Water Sanitization Temperatures" and §4-501.114 "Manual and Mechanical Warewashing Equipment, Chemical Sanitization". Capitalised words are terms defined in the Food Code. Priority markers (P, Pf) are left out of the quotes.

## FDA §4-702.11: sanitise after cleaning

FDA Food Code 2022, §4-702.11 says: "UTENSILS and FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES of EQUIPMENT shall be SANITIZED before use after cleaning."

## FDA §4-703.11(A) and §4-501.111: hot water sanitising by hand

FDA Food Code 2022, §4-703.11 says that after being cleaned, food-contact surfaces and utensils shall be sanitized in:

> "(A) Hot water manual operations by immersion for at least 30 seconds"

FDA Food Code 2022, §4-501.111 says: "If immersion in hot water is used for SANITIZING in a manual operation, the temperature of the water shall be maintained at 77°C (171°F) or above."

## FDA §4-703.11(B): hot water sanitising in a machine

FDA Food Code 2022, §4-703.11(B) covers hot water mechanical operations (dish machines). Items are cycled through the machine "achieving a UTENSIL surface temperature of 71°C (160°F) as measured by an irreversible registering temperature indicator".

## FDA §4-703.11(C): chemical sanitiser contact times

FDA Food Code 2022, §4-703.11(C) covers chemical sanitising "by immersion, manual swabbing, brushing, or pressure spraying methods". "Contact times shall be consistent with those on EPA-registered label use instructions by providing:"

> "(1) ... a contact time of at least 10 seconds for a chlorine solution specified under ¶ 4-501.114(A),"

> "(2) A contact time of at least 7 seconds for a chlorine solution of 50 MG/L that has a PH of 10 or less and a temperature of at least 38°C (100°F) or a PH of 8 or less and a temperature of at least 24°C (75°F),"

> "(3) A contact time of at least 30 seconds for other chemical SANITIZING solutions,"

## FDA §4-501.114(A): chlorine sanitiser temperatures

FDA Food Code 2022, §4-501.114(A) says: "A chlorine solution shall have a minimum temperature based on the concentration and PH of the solution as listed in the following chart". The chart, as printed:

| Concentration range (mg/L) | Minimum temperature, pH 10 or less | Minimum temperature, pH 8 or less |
|---|---|---|
| 25 – 49 | 49°C (120°F) | 49°C (120°F) |
| 50 – 99 | 38°C (100°F) | 24°C (75°F) |
| 100 | 13°C (55°F) | 13°C (55°F) |

## FDA §4-501.114(C): quaternary ammonium sanitiser

FDA Food Code 2022, §4-501.114(C) says a quaternary ammonium compound solution shall:

> "(1) Have a minimum temperature of 24°C (75°F),"

> "(2) Have a concentration as specified under § 7-204.11 and as indicated by the manufacturer's use directions included in the labeling, and"

> "(3) Be used only in water with 500 MG/L hardness or less or in water having a hardness no greater than specified by the EPA-registered label use instructions;"

§4-501.114 also says a chemical sanitizer "shall be used in accordance with the EPA-registered label use instructions".
