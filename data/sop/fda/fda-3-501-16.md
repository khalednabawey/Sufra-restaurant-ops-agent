# FDA Food Code 2022 §3-501.16: Hot and cold holding

Short verbatim extracts from the FDA Food Code 2022, §3-501.16 "Time/Temperature Control for Safety Food, Hot and Cold Holding". Capitalised words are terms defined in the Food Code. Priority markers (P, Pf) are left out of the quotes.

## FDA §3-501.16(A): hot holding and cold holding temperatures

FDA Food Code 2022, §3-501.16(A) says that, "Except during preparation, cooking, or cooling, or when time is used as the public health control as specified under §3-501.19", time/temperature control for safety (TCS) food "shall be maintained:"

> "(1) At 57°C (135°F) or above, except that roasts cooked to a temperature and for a time specified in ¶ 3-401.11(B) or reheated as specified in ¶ 3-403.11(E) may be held at a temperature of 54°C (130°F) or above; or"

> "(2) At 5°C (41°F) or less."

In short, under §3-501.16(A): hot holding is 57°C (135°F) or above, and cold holding is 5°C (41°F) or less.

## FDA §3-501.16(B): storing raw shell eggs

FDA Food Code 2022, §3-501.16(B) sets the storage temperature for raw shell eggs that have not been pasteurised:

> "(B) EGGS that have not been treated to destroy all viable Salmonellae shall be stored in refrigerated EQUIPMENT that maintains an ambient air temperature of 7°C (45°F) or less."

In short, under §3-501.16(B): untreated shell eggs go in refrigeration with an air temperature of 7°C (45°F) or less.
