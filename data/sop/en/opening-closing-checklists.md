# Opening and closing checklists

## Opening and closing times

Normal opening hours are 06:00–21:00. The opening checklist starts 30 minutes before opening time, at 05:30. The closing checklist finishes within 45 minutes after closing time, by 21:45. The supervisor on shift leads the checklist and signs it off. These checklists apply at all Sufra Café & Bakery branches.

## Ramadan opening and closing times

Ramadan hours are 08:00–16:00 (limited daytime service) and 17:00–01:00 (after iftar).

- Day service: the opening checklist starts at 07:30. The closing checklist finishes by 16:45.
- Night service: the opening checklist starts at 16:30. The closing checklist finishes by 01:45.

## Opening checklist: building and staff

1. Unlock, turn off the alarm and check for damage, leaks or signs of pests.
2. Turn on lights, POS, coffee machines and ovens.
3. Staff put on clean uniforms and hair covers, report any illness to the supervisor, and wash hands for at least 20 seconds.
4. Check that every handwashing sink has soap and paper towels.

## Opening checklist: temperatures and dates

1. Check and record every fridge, chiller and freezer in the temperature log. Cold food must be at 5°C (41°F) or less.
2. Check and record hot-holding units once they are in use. Hot food must be at 57°C (135°F) or above.
3. Check date labels on ready-to-eat food. Discard anything past Day 7, unlabelled or past the use-by date, and record it in the POS waste log as `expired`.
4. Report any temperature problem to the branch manager at once.

## Opening checklist: cleaning and food prep

1. Make up sanitiser at the strength on the label and check it.
2. Clean and sanitise counters, prep tables and display shelves.
3. Flush the coffee machine and check the grinder.
4. Bake off pastries from dough thawed in the fridge overnight.
5. Clean the seating area, tables and restrooms.

## Opening checklist: cash, POS and stock

1. Count the opening cash float and record it in the POS.
2. Check that POS prices match the menu boards, including any price change that takes effect today.
3. Check low-stock alerts. Below 2 days of cover, tell the branch manager, who must place an order or arrange a local top-up the same day.
4. The supervisor signs off the opening checklist. Doors open at 06:00 (08:00 and 17:00 in Ramadan).

## Closing checklist: food and waste

1. Close the doors at closing time: 21:00 (16:00 and 01:00 in Ramadan).
2. Record every discarded item in the POS waste log before the end of the shift, in g, ml or units, with a reason: `expired`, `prep_error`, `damaged` or `quality`.
3. Date-label ready-to-eat food that will be kept, and store it at 5°C (41°F) or less.
4. Move tomorrow's frozen croissant dough to the fridge to thaw. Never thaw on the counter.
5. Record closing temperatures in the temperature log.

## Closing checklist: cleaning

1. Clean and sanitise all food-contact surfaces, utensils and prep tables.
2. Clean the coffee machine, steam wands and grinder.
3. Wash, sanitise and air-dry all dishes.
4. Clean display chillers, ovens and the bakery area.
5. Sweep and mop floors. Clean the restrooms.
6. Empty bins and take the rubbish out.

## Closing checklist: cash and security

1. Count the cash drawer and compare it with the POS report. Record any difference and report it to the branch manager.
2. Store the cash in the safe.
3. Turn off ovens, coffee machines and lights. Keep fridges and freezers on.
4. Lock the doors and set the alarm.
5. The supervisor signs off the closing checklist. Closing must finish by 21:45 (16:45 and 01:45 in Ramadan).
