# Scheduling rules

## Scheduling rules: scope

These scheduling rules apply to all Sufra Café & Bakery branches: B1 Olaya, B2 Tahlia, B3 Smouha and B4 Al Malqa. Branch managers build the shift schedule for their own branch. Normal opening hours are 06:00–21:00. Ramadan hours are 08:00–16:00 (limited daytime service) and 17:00–01:00 (after iftar).

## Staffing target: orders per staff-hour

The staffing target is 15 orders per staff-hour. To plan staff for an hour, divide the expected orders by 15 and round up. For example, 70 expected orders in an hour ÷ 15 = 4.7, so schedule 5 staff for that hour. The minimum staffing rule still applies when orders are low.

## Minimum staffing on every shift

Every shift has at least 2 staff. One of them must be a supervisor. This minimum staffing rule applies to normal shifts and Ramadan shifts.

## Normal shift times

Shifts are 8 hours. There are two normal shifts:

- Morning shift: 06:00–14:00.
- Evening shift: 13:00–21:00.

The morning and evening shifts overlap from 13:00 to 14:00. Together they cover normal opening hours of 06:00–21:00.

## Ramadan shift times

During Ramadan, shifts follow the Ramadan opening hours:

- Day shift: 08:00–16:00 (limited daytime service).
- Night shift: 17:00–01:00 (after iftar).

Ramadan shifts are also 8 hours, with at least 2 staff and one supervisor on each shift.

## Ramadan schedule deadline

Branch managers must publish the Ramadan schedule one week before Ramadan starts. The Ramadan schedule uses the day shift (08:00–16:00) and the night shift (17:00–01:00).

## Labour cost target

The labour cost target is at most 25% of sales. Labour cost % = labour cost ÷ sales × 100.

If labour cost goes above 30% of sales, the branch manager escalates it to the area manager or the owner. For B1 Olaya and B4 Al Malqa, escalate to the Riyadh area manager. For B2 Tahlia (Jeddah) and B3 Smouha (Alexandria), which have no area manager, escalate to the owner.
