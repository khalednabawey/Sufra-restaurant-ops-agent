# Complaint handling

## Complaint handling on the spot

When a customer complains, staff acknowledge the complaint, apologise and fix it on the spot. Fixing a complaint can mean remaking the item, replacing it or giving a discount within the discount limits. This complaint handling rule applies at all Sufra Café & Bakery branches.

## Discount limits when fixing a complaint

When fixing a complaint, use the discount limits of the discount policy:

- Staff may give up to 10% off a single order for service recovery.
- Branch managers may give up to 25% off.
- Anything above 25% needs the owner.

Discounts never stack.

## Logging complaints in the POS

Every complaint is logged in the POS, including complaints fixed on the spot. Log what happened, what the customer said and how the complaint was fixed.

## Replying to online reviews

Every online review gets a reply within 48 hours.

## Food-safety complaints

A food-safety complaint goes to the branch manager immediately and to the owner within 24 hours. Examples: a foreign object in food, a customer who says they became ill after eating, an allergic reaction, or food that seems spoiled.

Keep the product sample. Do not throw it away. Seal it, label it with the date, time and complaint, and keep it in the fridge away from other food. Log the complaint in the POS.
