# Waste recording

## Waste recording: what and when to record

Every discarded item is recorded in the POS waste log before the end of each shift. This waste recording rule applies to all staff at all Sufra Café & Bakery branches. Record waste in g, ml or units. Examples: 250 ml of milk, 400 g of dough, 3 units of croissants.

## Waste reasons: the only four codes

The POS waste log has only four waste reasons:

- `expired`: the item passed its date mark or use-by date.
- `prep_error`: the item was made wrongly, over-produced, burnt or spilled during prep.
- `damaged`: delivery or packaging damage, or the item was dropped.
- `quality`: the item failed a quality check or was returned for quality.

No other waste reason is used.

## Choosing the waste reason: examples

- Milk past its date mark: `expired`.
- Croissants burnt in the oven: `prep_error`.
- Too many sandwiches made for the day: `prep_error`.
- Latte spilled while being made: `prep_error`.
- Cake dropped on the floor: `damaged`.
- Box of cups crushed in delivery: `damaged`.
- Pastry that failed a quality check: `quality`.
- Coffee returned by a customer because of its taste: `quality`.
- Chilled food found above 5°C (41°F) and discarded: `quality`.

## Waste target

The waste target is waste at or below 3% of stock used, by cost, per month. Stock used is everything that left stock: what went into products sold plus what was wasted. Waste % = cost of waste ÷ cost of stock used × 100. Example: SAR 900 of waste out of SAR 30,000 of stock used is 3%.

## Weekly waste above 5%

If waste goes above 5% of stock used, by cost, in any week, the branch manager sends a written explanation. For B1 Olaya and B4 Al Malqa, the written explanation goes to the Riyadh area manager. For B2 Tahlia (Jeddah) and B3 Smouha (Alexandria), which have no area manager, it goes to the owner.
