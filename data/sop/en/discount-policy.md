# Discount policy

## Discount policy: scope

This discount policy applies to all Sufra Café & Bakery branches: B1 Olaya, B2 Tahlia, B3 Smouha and B4 Al Malqa. It covers service recovery discounts, larger discounts and the staff meal discount.

## Discount limits by role

- Staff may give up to 10% off a single order for service recovery.
- Branch managers may give up to 25% off a single order.
- Any discount above 25% needs the owner's approval.

Only the owner can approve a discount above 25%. This applies to every role, including area managers.

## Service recovery discounts by staff

Staff may give up to 10% off a single order for service recovery, for example when an order was wrong, late or not up to standard. If the customer needs more than 10% off, staff call the branch manager. The branch manager may give up to 25% off. Above 25%, the owner must approve.

## Discounts never stack

Discounts never stack. Only one discount applies to an order. For example, a 10% service recovery discount cannot be added to another discount on the same order, and the staff meal discount cannot be combined with any other discount.

## Staff meal discount

Staff meals on shift are 50% off. The staff meal discount applies only to staff who are on shift. The 50% staff meal discount is a standing company rule and does not need owner approval. Like all discounts, it does not stack with any other discount.
