# Price change policy

## Price change policy: scope

This price change policy applies to menu and retail prices at all Sufra Café & Bakery branches: B1 Olaya (Riyadh), B2 Tahlia (Jeddah), B3 Smouha (Alexandria) and B4 Al Malqa (Riyadh). Prices at B1, B2 and B4 are in SAR. Prices at B3 Smouha are in EGP.

## Who can request a price change

Price changes are requested by an area manager or the owner. Branch managers and staff cannot request price changes.

Every price change request must include a written reason. A request without a written reason is not approved.

## Who approves a price change

Price changes are approved by the owner only. Area managers and branch managers cannot approve price changes, for any item or any amount. A price change is not valid until the owner approves it.

## When a price change takes effect

An approved price change takes effect from the next business day. For example, a price change approved on Sunday takes effect on Monday.

Menu boards and the POS must be updated before opening on the day the new price takes effect. Normal opening time is 06:00. During Ramadan, opening time is 08:00.

## Price change steps

1. The area manager or the owner writes the price change request: item, branch, current price, new price and a written reason.
2. The owner approves or rejects the price change.
3. The approved price takes effect from the next business day.
4. Before opening on that day, the branch updates the POS and the menu boards.
5. At opening, the opening checklist confirms that POS prices match the menu boards.
