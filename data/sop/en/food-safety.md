# Food safety rules

## Food safety rules: scope and source

These food safety rules apply to all Sufra Café & Bakery branches. The temperatures, times and steps in this document come from the FDA Food Code 2022. Each rule names the FDA section it comes from. TCS food means time/temperature control for safety food: food that needs time and temperature control to stay safe, such as milk, cream, cream-filled pastries, cut fruit and sandwiches.

## Cold holding temperature

Keep cold TCS food at 5°C (41°F) or less. This applies to fridges, under-counter chillers and display chillers. Source: FDA Food Code 2022, §3-501.16(A)(2).

Store raw shell eggs that have not been treated to destroy Salmonella in refrigeration with an air temperature of 7°C (45°F) or less. Source: FDA Food Code 2022, §3-501.16(B).

## Hot holding temperature

Keep hot TCS food at 57°C (135°F) or above, for example soups and hot sandwich fillings held for service. Source: FDA Food Code 2022, §3-501.16(A)(1).

## Temperature checks and the temperature log

Check and record the temperature of every fridge, chiller, freezer and hot-holding unit in the temperature log at opening and at closing.

If a cold-holding unit reads above 5°C (41°F), or hot-held food reads below 57°C (135°F), tell the branch manager at once and record the reading. If you cannot show that the food stayed safe, discard it and record it in the POS waste log with the reason `quality`.

## Date marking food made in the branch

Ready-to-eat TCS food made in the branch and kept for more than 24 hours gets a date label. Keep it at 5°C (41°F) or less and use it within a maximum of 7 days. The day of preparation is Day 1. Example: food made on 3 May (Day 1) must be used, sold or discarded by the end of 9 May (Day 7). Source: FDA Food Code 2022, §3-501.17(A).

## Date marking opened packages

When you open a package of ready-to-eat TCS food from a supplier and keep it for more than 24 hours, write the opening date on it. The day the package is opened is Day 1. The date you mark must never be later than the manufacturer's use-by date. Source: FDA Food Code 2022, §3-501.17(B).

When you combine date-marked ingredients, the new item keeps the date of the earliest-prepared ingredient. Source: FDA Food Code 2022, §3-501.17(C).

## Discarding date-marked food

Discard ready-to-eat TCS food if it is past its 7-day limit, if it has no date label, or if its label gives a date beyond the 7-day limit. Source: FDA Food Code 2022, §3-501.18(A). Record it in the POS waste log with the reason `expired`.

## Handwashing: how to wash hands

Wash hands and exposed arms for at least 20 seconds with soap at a handwashing sink. Source: FDA Food Code 2022, §2-301.12(A). Steps, from §2-301.12(B):

1. Rinse under clean, running warm water.
2. Apply soap.
3. Rub hands together hard for at least 10 to 15 seconds, including under the nails, the fingertips and between the fingers.
4. Rinse well under clean, running warm water.
5. Dry hands well straight away.

Use a paper towel to turn off the tap (§2-301.12(C)). Never wash hands in a food prep sink or dishwashing sink (§2-301.15).

## Handwashing: when to wash hands

Wash hands before starting food prep, and also (FDA Food Code 2022, §2-301.14):

- after using the toilet;
- after touching your face, hair or body;
- after coughing, sneezing, using a tissue, smoking, eating or drinking;
- after handling dirty equipment or utensils;
- when switching between raw food and ready-to-eat food;
- when changing tasks, as often as needed to remove dirt;
- before putting on gloves for a food task;
- after any other activity that contaminates the hands.

## Thawing frozen food

Thaw TCS food in one of these ways (FDA Food Code 2022, §3-501.13):

- In the fridge, with the food at 5°C (41°F) or less.
- Completely under running water at 21°C (70°F) or below, without letting thawed ready-to-eat parts rise above 5°C (41°F).
- As part of cooking, or in a microwave followed straight away by cooking.

Sufra company rule: thaw frozen croissant dough and other frozen products in the fridge. Never thaw on the counter at room temperature.

## Cleaning food-contact surfaces

Clean food-contact surfaces and utensils used with TCS food at least every 4 hours during the day. Source: FDA Food Code 2022, §4-602.11(C).

Also clean them when you change from raw food to ready-to-eat food, between raw fruit or vegetables and TCS food, before using or storing a food thermometer, and at any time they may have become contaminated. Source: FDA Food Code 2022, §4-602.11(A). Clean means clean to sight and touch (§4-601.11(A)).

## Sanitising with hot water

Sanitise food-contact surfaces and utensils after cleaning and before use (FDA Food Code 2022, §4-702.11).

- By hand: immerse in water at 77°C (171°F) or above for at least 30 seconds (§4-501.111 and §4-703.11(A)).
- In a hot water dish machine: the utensil surface must reach 71°C (160°F) (§4-703.11(B)).

## Sanitising with chemical sanitiser

Use chemical sanitiser as the label instructs (FDA Food Code 2022, §4-501.114).

- Chlorine at 50–99 mg/L: at least 24°C (75°F) if pH 8 or less, or at least 38°C (100°F) if pH 10 or less. Contact time at least 7 seconds at 50 mg/L in those conditions, otherwise at least 10 seconds (§4-501.114(A), §4-703.11(C)).
- Quaternary ammonium (quat): at least 24°C (75°F), at the label concentration, with a contact time of at least 30 seconds (§4-501.114(C), §4-703.11(C)).
