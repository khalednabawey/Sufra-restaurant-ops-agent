# Reorder policy

## Reorder policy: scope and branches

This reorder policy applies to all Sufra Café & Bakery branches: B1 Olaya (Riyadh), B2 Tahlia (Jeddah), B3 Smouha (Alexandria) and B4 Al Malqa (Riyadh, opened 1 April 2026). B1, B2 and B4 order in SAR. B3 Smouha orders in EGP. The Riyadh area covers B1 Olaya and B4 Al Malqa and has an area manager. Jeddah (B2 Tahlia) and Alexandria (B3 Smouha) currently have no area manager.

## Who can request a reorder

Branch managers and area managers can request reorders. Staff cannot request reorders. A staff member who notices low stock tells the branch manager.

A branch manager requests reorders for their own branch only. An area manager requests reorders for the branches in their area. The Riyadh area manager can request reorders for B1 Olaya and B4 Al Malqa.

## Reorder approval limits

Area managers approve reorders up to SAR 5,000 for branches in their area. The Riyadh area manager approves reorders up to SAR 5,000 for B1 Olaya and B4 Al Malqa. A reorder of exactly SAR 5,000 is within the area manager's limit.

The owner approves reorders above SAR 5,000.

The owner also approves all reorders, of any amount, for areas that have no area manager. These are currently Jeddah (B2 Tahlia) and Alexandria (B3 Smouha).

## No self-approval of reorders

Nobody approves their own reorder request. When the Riyadh area manager requests a reorder for B1 Olaya or B4 Al Malqa, the owner approves it, even if the amount is SAR 5,000 or less. A branch manager never approves a reorder; branch managers only request them.

## Reorder approval limits for EGP amounts

For the reorder approval limits, amounts in EGP are converted to SAR at a fixed rate of 13 EGP = 1 SAR. The SAR 5,000 limit equals EGP 65,000. For example, a reorder of EGP 52,000 equals SAR 4,000.

B3 Smouha is in Alexandria, which has no area manager, so the owner approves every B3 Smouha reorder, whatever the amount.

## Reorder approval examples

- B1 Olaya, SAR 3,200, requested by the branch manager: the Riyadh area manager approves.
- B4 Al Malqa, SAR 5,000, requested by the branch manager: the Riyadh area manager approves.
- B4 Al Malqa, SAR 7,500, requested by the branch manager: the owner approves, because it is above SAR 5,000.
- B2 Tahlia, SAR 1,000: the owner approves, because Jeddah has no area manager.
- B3 Smouha, EGP 20,000 (about SAR 1,538): the owner approves, because Alexandria has no area manager.
- B1 Olaya, SAR 2,000, requested by the Riyadh area manager: the owner approves, because nobody approves their own request.

## Reorder point formula

Reorder point = (supplier lead time + safety days) × average daily use over the last 14 days.

Average daily use = total use of the item in the last 14 days ÷ 14.

Place a reorder when the stock on hand plus stock already on order falls to or below the reorder point. Use the supplier lead times and safety days in this policy.

## Safety days for the reorder point

Safety days are 3 for perishables and 4 for everything else. A perishable is an item with a shelf life of 7 days or less, for example fresh milk. Items with a shelf life longer than 7 days, for example coffee beans, frozen croissant dough, tea, spices, packaging and retail goods, use 4 safety days.

## Supplier lead times for reorders

Supplier lead times used in the reorder point formula:

- Dairy: 1 day.
- Coffee roaster: 5 days.
- Bakery supplies: 2 days.
- Tea and spices: 7 days.
- Packaging: 10 days.
- Retail goods: 14 days.

## Order cycle: how much to reorder

Each reorder covers one order cycle. Order quantity = order cycle days × average daily use over the last 14 days.

Order cycles:

- Fresh milk: 3 days.
- Frozen croissant dough: 7 days.
- Coffee beans: 7 days.
- Everything else: 14 days.

## Reorder worked examples

Fresh milk (dairy, perishable): lead time 1 day + 3 safety days = 4 days. At an average use of 40 litres a day, the reorder point is 4 × 40 = 160 litres. One order covers 3 days: 3 × 40 = 120 litres.

Coffee beans (coffee roaster): lead time 5 days + 4 safety days = 9 days. At 6 kg a day, the reorder point is 9 × 6 = 54 kg. One order covers 7 days: 42 kg.

Frozen croissant dough (bakery supplies): lead time 2 days + 4 safety days = 6 days. At 120 pieces a day, the reorder point is 720 pieces. One order covers 7 days: 840 pieces.

## Low-stock alert

A low-stock alert is raised when an item falls below 2 days of cover. Days of cover = stock on hand ÷ average daily use over the last 14 days. Example: 50 litres of fresh milk at 40 litres a day is 1.25 days of cover, which is below 2 days.

When there is a low-stock alert, the branch manager must place an order or arrange a local top-up the same day. The normal reorder approval limits apply to the order.
